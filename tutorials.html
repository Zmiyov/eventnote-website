How to use Events Notes

Short, practical guides to every feature — from your first booking to Sun Track, backups and team.

EN UK RU
🚀

Getting started

Set the app up for the way you work and add your first job.

🎭Pick your roleYour role tailors event types and which tools appear.

  1. Open Settings and tap “Your role”.

  2. Choose Photographer, Videographer, Agency, DJ, Decorator, Event Host, Makeup Artist, Hairdresser or Catering.

  3. Event types and tools like Sun Track adjust to your role automatically.

💡Tip: You can change your role anytime — your existing events stay.
📅Create a bookingAdd an event with client, type, date, place and notes.

  1. On the Schedule tab, pick a date and tap Add (+).

  2. Choose the kind of shooting and set the date and time.

  3. Add the client, location, price and any notes.

  4. Save — the event appears on that day in your schedule.

💡Tip: First launch? The guided onboarding builds your first event with you.
🗂️

Managing bookings

Everything inside an event, and how to stay on top of your schedule.

💰Payments, prepayment & certificatesTrack full price, deposits and gift certificates per event.

  1. In an event, set the full price for the job.

  2. Add the Prepayment (deposit) the client has already paid.

  3. Mark a booking as a gift certificate when it is paid upfront as a gift.

  4. Totals feed into your Statistics automatically.

💡Tip: The remaining balance is simply the full price minus the prepayment.
🗓️Calendar & scheduleBrowse your month and jump to any day’s events.

  1. The Schedule tab shows a month calendar with marked days.

  2. Tap any date to see that day’s events below.

  3. Swipe between months to plan ahead.

  4. Tap an event to open details, or swipe a row to edit or delete it.

⏳Deadlines & countdownsSee what’s unfinished and how many days are left.

  1. Open the Deadlines tab to see unfinished jobs.

  2. Each one shows the days left until its deadline.

  3. Set a deadline (and a deadline reminder) when creating or editing an event.

  4. Long-press a job to mark it ready.

📋Timeline & tasksBreak a shoot into timed stages and to-dos.

  1. Open an event and go to the Timeline section.

  2. Add timeline stages with a time and description for each part of the day.

  3. Add tasks (“Add a task”) for things to prepare or bring.

  4. Check off tasks as you complete them.

🖼️Photo galleryAttach reference and inspiration photos to an event.

  1. Open an event and find the Gallery section.

  2. Add photos from your library — poses, moodboards, client references.

  3. Tap a photo to view it full-screen on the shoot day.

👤Client contactsKeep every client’s links and reach them in one tap.

  1. The Contacts tab lists everyone you’ve saved.

  2. Save phone, Telegram, Instagram and website for a client.

  3. Tap the phone number to call; tap Telegram or Instagram to open the app.

  4. When creating an event, you can pick a client from your phone’s contacts.

🔔RemindersLocal notifications for events and deadlines — no account needed.

  1. When creating or editing an event, set a reminder time.

  2. Set a separate deadline reminder for delivery dates.

  3. Allow notifications when the app asks.

  4. Reminders fire on your device — nothing is sent to a server.

🧭

Planning tools

Plan light and travel before you arrive.

☀️Sun TrackPlan golden hour and natural light for any spot and date.

  1. Open Sun Track (available for photographer, videographer, agency, decorator and event-host roles).

  2. Move the map so your shoot spot sits under the center pin.

  3. Pick the date and time to see the sun’s position and path.

  4. Use it to plan sunrise, sunset and golden-hour shots.

💡Tip: Sun Track needs location access to calculate the sun’s path.
📍Locations & Create RouteSave a shoot spot and open directions in one tap.

  1. Add a location to an event by searching or placing it on the map.

  2. Open the event details and tap the location.

  3. Tap “Create route” to open directions in Maps.

📊

Insights

See how your business is really doing.

📈AnalyticsEarnings, sessions and hours at a glance.

  1. Open the Statistics tab.

  2. See earnings, number of sessions and hours by month.

  3. Spot your busiest seasons and top services.

Sync, import & backup

Keep your data safe and bring in events you already have.

☁️iCloud syncYour data, in sync across iPhone, iPad and Mac.

  1. Sign in to iCloud on your device and enable iCloud Drive.

  2. In Settings → Data synchronization, check the status shows “On”.

  3. Your events then sync across all your Apple devices automatically.

📆Import from Apple CalendarBring events in from the Apple Calendar.

  1. Open Settings → Import from Apple Calendar.

  2. Allow calendar access.

  3. Pick the events to bring in as bookings.

🔄Import from Google CalendarBring existing events in from Google Calendar.

  1. Open Settings → Import from Google Calendar.

  2. Sign in to your Google account.

  3. Choose events to import into Events Notes.

🛟Backups & restoreSave snapshots and restore data safely.

  1. In Settings, open the backups (snapshots) screen.

  2. Create a snapshot to save a copy of your data.

  3. To restore, choose “Restore missing data” to merge, or “Replace everything” to overwrite.

💡Tip: “Replace everything” overwrites current data — use it only on a fresh device.

More

Widgets, team, Mac and your subscription.

🧩Home Screen widgetUpcoming events, always one glance away.

  1. Long-press your Home Screen and tap the + in the corner.

  2. Search for Events Notes and pick a widget size.

  3. Add it to see your upcoming events at a glance.

👥Team membersAdd your team to an event — for the Agency role.

  1. Switch to the Agency role to use team features.

  2. In an event, open the Team section and tap “Add team member”.

  3. Assign a contact and add info for each member on that shoot.

💻The Mac appManage bookings full-screen on your Mac.

  1. Install Events Notes on your Mac from the App Store.

  2. Sign in to the same iCloud account.

  3. Manage bookings full-screen — everything stays in sync.

⭐PremiumWhat a subscription unlocks.

  1. Free accounts can keep up to 3 upcoming events (5 in Ukraine).

  2. Premium removes the limit and unlocks all features.

  3. Choose monthly or yearly billing.
