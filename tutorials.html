How to use Events Notes

Short, practical guides to every feature — from your first booking to Sun Track, backups and team.

EN UK RU

Your month at a glance, and how to add a booking.

  • Calendar & scheduleUse the calendar to see the events of any day.4 steps· Schedule › Calendar
    1. The Schedule tab has a calendar in month or week view with special marks on the days. Red marks today, blue the selected day, orange a day that’s unavailable for bookings, and a small dot under the number shows that there are bookings on that date.
    2. Tap any date to see that day’s events below.
    3. Swipe to scroll through the months.
    4. Tap an event to open its details; swipe a row right to edit it or left to delete it.
  • Create a new entryAdd an event with all its details.4 steps· Schedule › Calendar
    1. On the Schedule tab, pick a date and tap Add (+).
    2. Choose the kind of shooting and set the date and time.
    3. Add the details of the client, location, price and notes.
    4. Save — the event appears on that day in your schedule.

Everything inside a booking: money, plans, light and the route.

  • Payments, prepayment & certificatesTrack full price, deposits and gift certificates per event.4 steps· Schedule › Event
    1. In an event, set the full price for the job.
    2. Add the Prepayment (deposit) the client has already paid.
    3. Mark a booking as a gift certificate when it is paid upfront as a gift.
    4. The amount in the full price field is counted on the Statistics page automatically.
  • TimelineCreate a schedule that breaks a shoot into timed stages.2 steps· Schedule › Event
    1. Open an event and go to the Timeline section.
    2. Add timeline stages with a time and description for each part of the day.
  • To-do listCreate a separate to-do list for each shoot.3 steps· Schedule › Event
    1. Open an event and go to the To Do tab.
    2. Add tasks (“Add a task”) for things to prepare or bring.
    3. Check off completed tasks.
  • Photo galleryAdd reference and inspiration photos to an event.4 steps· Schedule › Event
    1. Open an event and find the Gallery section.
    2. Add photos from your library — poses, moodboards, client references.
    3. Tap a photo to view it full-screen on the shoot day.
    4. Mark photos as used.
  • RemindersSet up local notifications for events and deadlines.4 steps· Schedule › Event
    1. When creating or editing an event, set a reminder time for when it starts.
    2. Set a separate deadline reminder for delivery dates.
    3. Allow notifications when the app asks.
    4. Reminders appear on your device as push notifications.
  • Sun TrackPick the time with the best light for any spot and date.4 steps· Schedule › Event
    1. Open Sun Track (on the main screen or the event details screen).
    2. Move the map so your shoot spot sits under the center pin.
    3. Pick the date and time.
    4. Plan shots at sunrise, sunset or golden hour.
  • Locations & Create RouteSave a shoot spot and open directions in one tap.3 steps· Schedule › Event
    1. Add a location to an event by searching or placing it on the map.
    2. Open the event details and tap the location.
    3. Tap “Create route” to open directions in Maps.
  • Team membersAdd your team to an event — for the Agency role.2 steps· Schedule › Event
    1. In the new event form, open the Team section and tap “Add team member”.
    2. Assign a contact and add info for each member on that shoot.

Set the app up for the way you work, and keep your data safe.

  • Pick your roleYour role tailors event types and which tools appear.3 steps· Schedule › Settings
    1. Open Settings and tap “Your role”.
    2. Choose Photographer, Videographer, Agency, DJ, Decorator, Event Host, Makeup Artist, Hairdresser or Catering.
    3. Event types and tools like Sun Track adjust to your role automatically.

    Tip: You can change your role anytime — your existing events stay.

  • iCloud syncYour data, in sync across iPhone, iPad and Mac.3 steps· Schedule › Settings
    1. Sign in to iCloud on your device and enable iCloud Drive.
    2. In Settings → Data synchronization, check the status shows “On”.
    3. Your events then sync across all your Apple devices automatically.
  • Import from Apple CalendarBring events in from the Apple Calendar.3 steps· Schedule › Settings
    1. Open Settings → Import from Apple Calendar.
    2. Allow calendar access.
    3. Pick the events to bring in as bookings.
  • Import from Google CalendarBring existing events in from Google Calendar.3 steps· Schedule › Settings
    1. Open Settings → Import from Google Calendar.
    2. Sign in to your Google account.
    3. Choose events to import into Events Notes.
  • Backups & restoreSave snapshots and restore data safely.2 steps· Schedule › Settings
    1. In Settings, open the backups (snapshots) screen.
    2. To restore, choose “Restore missing data” to merge, or “Replace everything” to overwrite.

    Tip: “Replace everything” overwrites your current data. Read the instructions to make the right choice.

  • PremiumWhat a subscription unlocks.3 steps· Schedule › Settings
    1. Free accounts can keep up to 3 upcoming events (5 in Ukraine).
    2. Premium removes the limit and unlocks all features.
    3. Choose monthly or yearly billing.

Every delivery counted down, and off your list once it’s done.

  • Deadlines & countdownsSee what’s unfinished and how many days are left.3 steps· Deadlines
    1. Open the Deadlines tab to see unfinished jobs.
    2. Each one shows the days left until its deadline — urgent ones turn red.
    3. Set a deadline (and a deadline reminder) when creating or editing an event.
  • Mark a job as doneDelivered it? Take it off your deadlines list.3 steps· Deadlines
    1. Open the Deadlines tab.
    2. Press and hold the job you’ve delivered.
    3. Tap Done — it’s off your list.

Clients and colleagues, one tap away.

  • Every client in one listClients from your events are saved for you.2 steps· Contacts
    1. The Contacts tab lists everyone you’ve saved.
    2. In Colleagues, save the contacts of people you work with, so you can quickly get in touch or add them to a team.
  • Reach a client in a tapCall, message or open Instagram from their card.3 steps· Contacts
    1. Find the client in Contacts and open their card.
    2. Tap the phone number to call.
    3. Tap Telegram or Instagram to open the app.

See how your year is really going.

  • Review your results for the year or any other periodSee your earnings, number of shoots and hours shot for different periods.3 steps· Statistics
    1. Open the Statistics tab.
    2. See earnings, number of sessions and hours by month.
    3. Spot your busiest seasons and top services.
  • Build custom chartsChart what matters to your business.4 steps· Statistics
    1. Open the Statistics tab.
    2. Under “Create custom charts”, tap Create.
    3. Choose the chart type, how to group it and what to show, then tap “Create chart”.
    4. Your charts sit on the Statistics screen — like shoots by type for a quarter.

Events Notes on your Home Screen and on your Mac.

  • Home Screen widgetUpcoming events, always one glance away.3 steps· Beyond the app
    1. Long-press your Home Screen and tap the + in the corner.
    2. Search for Events Notes and pick a widget size.
    3. Add it to see your upcoming events at a glance.
  • The Mac appManage bookings full-screen on your Mac.3 steps· Beyond the app
    1. Install Events Notes on your Mac from the App Store.
    2. Sign in to the same iCloud account.
    3. Manage bookings full-screen — everything stays in sync.
