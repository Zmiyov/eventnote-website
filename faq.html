Questions and answers

What is Events Notes?

Events Notes is an app for iPhone, iPad and Mac for planning and running shoots and events. Each booking keeps the client, location, price and deposit, tasks, references and delivery deadline together, and the app adds up your earnings, sessions and hours automatically.

Who is Events Notes for?

Photographers, videographers and other event professionals. You pick your role — Photographer, Videographer, Agency, DJ, Decorator, Event Host, Makeup Artist, Hairdresser or Catering — and event types and tools adjust to it.

Is Events Notes free?

Yes, it's free to download. A free account can keep up to 3 upcoming events (5 in Ukraine). Premium removes the limit and unlocks all features, including iCloud sync. You can pay monthly or yearly.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later, and Mac. The Mac app is a free companion, and with iCloud sync your events are the same on every device.

Can I import my events from Google Calendar or Apple Calendar?

Yes. Events Notes imports existing events from both Apple Calendar and Google Calendar, so you don't have to type your schedule again.

Can it track payments and deposits?

Yes. Every event stores the full price, the prepayment (deposit) and whether it's a gift certificate. These amounts feed your statistics, so you see what you've earned each month and year.

What is Sun Track?

Sun Track shows the sun's path over your shoot location on the event date, with a golden-hour timeline, so you can plan outdoor shoots around the light.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, it syncs through your own iCloud account, and you can make backups and restore them. Events Notes doesn't use your data for advertising and never sells it.

Which languages is the app available in?

The app is available in 18 languages, including English, Ukrainian, Russian, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Polish, Portuguese and Japanese.

Ready to organize your next shoot?

Download Events Notes and bring your bookings, schedule, and clients together.
