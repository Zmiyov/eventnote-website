# Product

## Platform

web

This repository is the marketing website (www.eventsnotes.top) for a native Apple app. The app itself runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac; the site is the web surface.

## Users

Primary: working photographers and videographers, mostly solo or in small teams, who book shoots with clients (weddings, love stories, families, portraits, events) and today juggle bookings, deposits and delivery deadlines in a notebook, Notes app or calendar. They visit the site deciding whether the app is worth downloading.

Secondary: other event professionals the app supports through its role setting: Agency, DJ, Decorator, Event Host, Makeup Artist, Hairdresser, Catering. The site leads with photographers and acknowledges these roles; it does not speak to them equally.

Markets: English-speaking visitors and Ukrainian/Russian-speaking visitors matter equally. The site ships in English, Ukrainian (`uk/`) and Russian (`ru/`).

## Product Purpose

Events Notes keeps every shoot, client, payment and delivery deadline in one place, and turns the bookings into yearly statistics automatically. Success for the site is a visitor who understands what the app does for their shoot workflow and taps through to the App Store.

## Positioning

- **A native Apple app, not a web CRM.** Real iPhone, iPad and Mac apps synced through iCloud.
- **Shoot-day tools inside each booking.** Sun Track shows the sun's path and golden hour over the location on the event date; one tap opens the route in Apple Maps.
- **Solo-sized and simple.** A notebook replacement for one person or a small crew, not a heavy business suite like HoneyBook, Dubsado or Studio Ninja.
- **Free to start, cheap to keep.** Free for up to 3 upcoming events (5 in Ukraine); Premium removes the limit, billed monthly or yearly.

## Operating Context

A booking's life: create the event (type, date, hours, deadline, client contacts), add references and moodboards, location and price/deposit, assign team members, plan to-do tasks; on the day check the sun and route; afterwards track the delivery deadline countdown and mark it done; over the year read earnings, sessions and hours in Statistics. The home page tells this as a guided tour of one shoot (Create → Details → Deadlines → Statistics → Contacts).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- App features: bookings, payments (full price, prepayment, gift certificates), deadlines with days-left countdown, timeline and to-do per event, reference gallery, client and colleague contacts, team members, Sun Track, locations and routes, analytics with custom charts, event and deadline reminders, Apple and Google Calendar import, iCloud sync, backups and restore, Home Screen widget, macOS app.
- App languages: 18 (per `llms.txt`). Requires iOS 18 or later.
- Site stack: plain static HTML/CSS/JS on GitHub Pages. `uk/`, `ru/` and the guides in `tutorials.html` are generated by `node tools/build-pages.mjs`; edit the sources (`index.html`, `tutorials.html`, `flow.js`, `tutorials.js`) and rebuild, never the generated pages.
- Every user-facing string must exist in all three languages.
- Open decisions: canonical product name (site "Events Notes", App Store "Event Notes: Shoot Planner CRM", Mac wordmark "Event Note"); a pricing/Premium section on the site.

## Product Principles

1. Show the app working on a real shoot, not a feature list: the booking-to-statistics story is the argument.
2. Photographers first, without shutting out the other roles.
3. Lead with what a web CRM can't do: native Apple apps, and shoot-day tools in the booking.
4. Keep the promise solo-sized: simple, free to start, nothing that implies a heavy suite.
5. Every claim must be true in all three languages and match the current app build.
