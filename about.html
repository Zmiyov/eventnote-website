About Events Notes

Events Notes is an app for people who earn their living at events — photographers, videographers, makeup artists, DJs, decorators, hosts and agencies. It keeps every booking, client, payment and deadline in one place on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Who makes it

Events Notes is designed and built by Volodymyr Pysarenko, an independent iOS developer.

Since 2023

The first version came out on the App Store in January 2023, and the app is still updated regularly. Today it covers both planning a shoot and the business side of the work: prices and deposits, delivery deadlines, team members, statistics with custom charts, Sun Track for golden hour, and a native Mac app.

At a glance

  • First released: January 2023
  • Platforms: iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later), Mac
  • App languages: 18, including English, Ukrainian and Russian
  • Price: free for up to 3 upcoming events (5 in Ukraine); Premium removes the limit, billed monthly or yearly
  • Roles: Photographer, Videographer, Agency, DJ, Decorator, Event Host, Makeup Artist, Hairdresser, Catering
  • Developer: Volodymyr Pysarenko

Your data

Your events are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, they sync through your own iCloud account. Events Notes doesn’t use your data for advertising and never sells it. The details are in the Privacy Policy.
