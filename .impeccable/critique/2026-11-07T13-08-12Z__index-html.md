## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment: category-interchangeable. Centred tracked-caps eyebrow, two-line H1 with forced <br>, grey lead, badge + text link, numbered stepper: swap the H1 and it sells an invoicing app. The H1 leads with statistics, the least distinctive feature. Specific to the product: the warm amber palette and the words booking/shoot/deliver. Missing from the first screen: any app screen, Sun Track / golden hour, the word "photographer", "free".

Deterministic scan: 33 findings on index.html. In the hero/first viewport: header `.nav-cta` "Download" and active `.lang-btn` white on #c97b4a 3.27:1 (fails AA); hero eyebrow #a85f33 on #f7f4ed 4.40:1 (marginal fail); "Start the tour ↓" same 4.40:1; hero-eyebrow-chip / kicker-above-heading on the eyebrow; cream-palette; mobile straggler "grow." in the lead. Outside the hero: 15 cramped-padding, 7 buried-raster, nested-cards, bounce-easing, stripes, all inside the tour phone mock and false positives (recreated iOS UI; opacity-0 images are JS-revealed states). Step-card low contrast (1.3–1.6:1) is the intentional dimming of inactive tour steps.

Agreement: both flag the eyebrow as a template trope. Detector caught the header CTA contrast the design review missed; design review caught the mobile header overlap and stepper overflow, which the detector has no rule for.
